# Clackety
## introduction
A simple typing speed tester I made to improve my typing speed. It gives you random words to type and tracks your WPM, accuracy, raw WPM, and characters typed.

It keeps track of:
- words per minute
- accuracy
- raw words per minute
- amount of characters typed

you can also choose how long you want to type for.
